* <b>Reminds you periodically to check/flip the food while it tracks the total cooking time.</b>
  Interval timer + stopwatch = periodic alarms + elapsed time.
* <b>Quick access on the lock screen, home screen, and notifications without unlocking your phone.</b>
  Pull-down notification, lock screen notification, home screen widget, lock screen widget, and use the app over the lock screen (on supported devices).
* Adjust alarms while the timer is running.
* Create a quick-access menu with frequent interval times and your own notes.
* No ads.

<br>Example intervals:

* <i>10</i> = 10 minutes
* <i>7:30</i> = 7 minutes, 30 seconds
* <i>3:15:00</i> = 3 hours, 15 minutes

<br>Short forms:

* <i>12:00</i> = <i>12:0</i> = <i>12:</i> = <i>12</i> = 12 minutes
* <i>0:09</i> = <i>:9</i> = 9 seconds
* <i>2:00:00</i> = <i>2:0:0</i> = <i>2::</i> = <i>120</i> = 2 hours

Enter the interval time in <i>minutes</i>, <i>minutes:seconds</i>, or <i>hours:minutes:seconds</i> (MM, MM:SS, HH:MM:SS).

<br><b>Tips</b>

* <b>Run</b> / <b>Pause</b> / <b>Stop</b> the timer on a Wear OS smartwatch via the app's notification, but Wear OS doesn't show the ticking timer.
* Tap the checkbox to turn periodic reminder alarms on/off.
* Tap the time display to cycle between <i>stopped</i> → <i>running</i> → <i>paused</i> → <i>stopped</i>.
* To use the app over the lock screen: While viewing the app’s activity, tap the power button to turn off the screen, then tap the power button to wake the screen.
* BBQ Timer home screen widget:
    * Add it: Long-press the Home screen app icon, tap Widgets in the menu, then tap or drag the BBQ Timer widget.
    * Tap the widget’s ⏸️/▶️ button or elapsed time to <i>run</i>/<i>pause</i>/<i>stop</i> the timer.
    * Tap its background or countdown to open the app.
    * Long-press it then drag its handles to see more/less info.
* While BBQ Timer is <i>running</i> or <i>paused</i>, it's <b>on the lock screen</b> and <b>in the pull-down notification</b>.
    * To get the notification onto the lock screen: tap ⏸️or ▶️in the app or widget.
    * Long-press the app’s home screen icon, then tap the “Pause at 00:00” shortcut to <i>pause</i> it.
* The app, home/lock screen widget, and pull-down notification show the countdown interval time and the total elapsed time.
* Tap ▲ in the alarm interval field to open the menu of Alarm intervals.
    * Tap “Edit these intervals…” in the menu (or long-press ▲) to edit the menu.
    * Each line should have a duration in the form <i>HH:MM:SS</i> or <i>MM:SS</i> or <i>MM</i>, followed by optional notes. E.g. “6 thin fish, cook to 145°F” to cook 6 minutes/side and check it for 145°F.
    * In the editor, tap the “Reset” command to build a fresh menu of intervals using the current Temperature units (Android 14+) or language/country.
* In the app, the phone’s volume keys adjust the Alarm volume.

<br><b>System settings for reliable alarms</b>

* Alarm volume at an audible level.
* Lock screen → Show non-private or all notifications.
* Settings → Apps → Special app access → Alarms & reminders → Allowed.
* Apps → BBQ Timer “Show notifications” <i>not</i> Silent. (Optional: Override Do Not Disturb.)
* Apps → BBQ Timer “Alarm” notification category → “Show notifications”; <i>not</i> “Silent”; “Make sound and pop on screen”; sound choice <i>not</i> “None”; Importance “High” or higher to hear and see it on the lock screen and in the notification area.
* Notifications → App notifications → BBQ Timer → On.
